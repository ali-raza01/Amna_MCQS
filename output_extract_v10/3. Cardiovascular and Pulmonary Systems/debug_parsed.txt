Question 187
Congestive Heart Failure is referred to the Physical Therapy Department for gait training.
Which assessment test is the most reliable to calculate exercise capacity
1. Chair-stand
2. 6-minute walk
3. Time up and go
4. Physical performance
Correct Answer: 2
rationale:
Exercise capacity can be assessed by the following tests :Maximal test (exercise stress test;
EST),Cardiopulmonary exercise test (CPET),Submaximal test (six-minute walk test; 6MWT),-
Submaximal treadmill test and Incremental shuttle walk test (ISWT).
The six-minute walk test (6MWT is a self-paced walking test well suited to a hospital or commu-
nity setting.
For patients with more severe classes of heart disease, 6MWT results correlate well with VO2
peak, and only one 6MWT is recommended.
In addition to measuring exercise capacity, more specific functional testing can be used. Some
examples such as Timed up and go test. More recently, combined measures such as the Short
Physical Performance Battery (standing balance, walking speed, and chair stand) and the Func-
tional Difficulty Questionnaire have been validated in cardiac surgery patients.
