### Q1 — Most accurate body composition assessment
**Question:** Which is widely considered the most accurate body composition assessment?

**Chosen answer:** **1 — Hydrostatic weighing**

**Confidence:** High

**Reasoning:**
- Hydrostatic weighing is a classic criterion method for body composition.

### Q2 — Minimal blood glucose too high for exercise
**Question:** Minimal blood glucose considered too high for a diabetic patient to begin exercise?

**Chosen answer:** **1 — 300 mg/dl**

**Confidence:** High

**Reasoning:**
- Exercise is generally avoided when glucose is very high, especially >300 mg/dL, particularly with ketones.
- 400 mg/dL is even higher but 300 is the commonly used threshold.
