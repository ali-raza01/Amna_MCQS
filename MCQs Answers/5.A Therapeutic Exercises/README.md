### Q1 — Walking on slippery surface
**Question:** Patient walking on slippery surface — what to teach?

**Chosen answer:** **2 — Wide base of support**

**Confidence:** High

**Reasoning:**
- A wider base of support increases stability and reduces fall risk on slippery surfaces.
- Walking fast increases slip risk; keeping COG close helps but is less specific than widening base.

**Self-check:** Pass.

### Q2 — Knee convex-concave rule in open chain
**Question:** Convex-concave rule of the knee joint in open chain movement.

**Chosen answer:** **1 — Tibia roll and glide on the femur in the same direction**

**Confidence:** High

**Reasoning:**
- In open chain, the tibia moves on the femur.
- Tibia is concave, femur is convex; for concave-on-convex, roll and glide occur in the same direction.

**Self-check:** Pass.
